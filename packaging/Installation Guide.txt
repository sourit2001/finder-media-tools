RightClick Converter 0.7

System requirements: Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later.

Installation:
1. Double-click “RightClick Converter Installer.pkg”.
2. If macOS prevents it from opening, go to:
   System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
3. After installation, select a video or audio file in Finder.
4. Right-click a video and choose Extract Audio As…, or right-click an audio file and choose Convert Audio To…; then select MP3, M4A, or WAV.

To compress a video, choose Compress Video → Quick Compress, Under 20 MB, Under 50 MB, Under 100 MB, or Custom Size…. A small progress window lets you cancel and show finished files in Finder. Custom Size also offers resolution and audio options. Each selected video receives its own size limit.

Audio conversion does not open an additional window. The converted file will be created next to the original file.
If the right-click menu does not appear, go to “System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → File Providers”,
make sure Finder Audio Tools is enabled, then reopen the Finder window.

The first 5 conversions are free. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited use. All conversions are performed locally on your Mac, and your files are never uploaded.
This is a test version that has not yet been signed or notarized with an Apple Developer ID.

Uninstallation: Move “RightClick Converter.app” to the Trash.
